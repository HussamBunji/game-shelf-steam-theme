Three steps

How it works

  1. 1

    Download

    Click the big button above. You get one small file.

  2. 2

    Run the installer

    Open the file. It also installs Millennium, the free open-source loader, if you don't have it, then restarts Steam for you.

  3. 3

    Choose your themes

    Tick the looks you like. Switch between them any time from the THEMES button in Steam.
